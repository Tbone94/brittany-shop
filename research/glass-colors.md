## 2. Visual traits

- **Light in transparent glass:** color comes from absorption, so thick sections look darker and sometimes shift hue ("volume color"). Thin sections look lighter. This is the reverse of the "saturated in thin areas" idea in your brief. Form also creates light accents (RCA thesis, Bullseye note on very dark glass). I found no source on caustics or colored shadows, so treat those as unverified.
- **Surface decoration:** dots, stringers, and twisties (cane of twisted colors, also called zanfirico). Also frit (crushed glass), murrine (cane cross-sections), millefiori (many flower murrine), latticino (white-cane work) and gold or silver foil.
- **Encasement:** a clear layer over decoration. Sources only touch it via course listings. I found no source on how it changes the look.
- **Silver and ivory reactions:** silver forms tiny particles that scatter light, giving rainbow, blue and mauve shifts. SIS (silvered ivory stringer) makes organic surface veins. Reichenbach "Iris" and "Multicolour" strike or reduce to metallic.
- **Naming and grouping:** by family, then handle. Effetre groups: Transparent, Opaque, Opalino, Alabaster, Specials, "Cool Colors". Reichenbach: Transparent, Opaque, Mystics and Pearls. CiM: opaque, semi-opaque, translucent, opal, plus limited runs and "uniques". Names are playful (Smurfy, Dirty Martini, Sangre). Czech beads pair a base with a finish: "Turquoise Opaque, Picasso".

| Thai Orchid | #8A4FBF | White 5.3 |

Each accent passes in only one direction; the other direction fails.

**Splash combos.** Layer them with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` at about 75% opacity:
1. **Hot Stove:** Uranium Yellow + Sunset Coral + Rubino Berry (yellow over coral overlaps to orange).
2. **Lagoon:** Smurfy Turquoise + Cobalt, optionally + Dark Emerald (deep sapphire overlap).
3. **Spring Pour:** Uranium Yellow + Smurfy Turquoise (green overlap).
4. **Berry Glaze:** Thai Orchid + Rubino Berry + Sunset Coral.

## 4. Motifs

1. **Overlapping translucent layers:** 2-3 blurred blobs behind each product photo, blended by multiply.
2. **Raised-dot pattern:** dot grids with a small highlight, as section dividers and card corners.
3. **Stringer lines:** thin wavy 1-2px lines in accent colors under headings and links.
4. **Twisty stripes:** a diagonal two-color candy-twist band for sale banners and progress bars.
5. **Millefiori badges:** small circular concentric-ring marks for "new" or "handmade" tags and the favicon.
6. **Silvered shimmer:** a subtle gradient sheen on hover over buttons, echoing silver-glass reactions.
