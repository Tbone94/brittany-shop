Privacy

Short version: we only ask for what's needed to answer you and send your necklace, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Your email, if you join the list (“First dibs on new batches”). It's only used to tell you about new necklaces. Every email has a way to unsubscribe.
  • What you send in a form (getting in touch, asking about a piece, asking for one like it): your name, email and message, so Brittany can write back.
  • Order details when you buy: your name, shipping address and email, to send the necklace and its tracking number.

Who else handles it

  • Payments are handled by the payment company. Your card number goes straight to them and never reaches us.
  • Forms are delivered by email through FormSubmit, a form service.
  • Shipping: your address goes to USPS to deliver the package.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your browser's address when the page opens.

That's it: no ads, no tracking cookies, and we don't sell or share your information with anyone else.

Your bag

The necklaces in your bag are saved in your own browser so they're still there next time. Nothing about your bag is sent to us until you check out.

Changing your mind

Want your email off the list or your messages deleted? Get in touch and it's done.
